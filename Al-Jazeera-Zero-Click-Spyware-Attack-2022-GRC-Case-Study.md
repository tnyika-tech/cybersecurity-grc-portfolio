# **Al Jazeera Zero-Click Spyware Attack 2022: Independent GRC Case Study of Reported Attacks Targeting Journalists**

## **1. Project Overview**

This project examines the zero-click spyware incident involving Al Jazeera journalists, using publicly available cybersecurity research and reporting.



## **7. Research Ethics and Limitations**

This project is conducted independently for educational and professional portfolio purposes. It relies exclusively on publicly available cybersecurity research, technical publications and credible media reporting

## **The following ethical boundaries apply:**

- No penetration testing, vulnerability scanning or unauthorised security testing will be conducted against Al Jazeera or its associated infrastructure.
- No attempts will be made to access private systems , accounts, networks or devices belonging to Al Jazeera or its journalists or employees
- The project will not disclose confidential information, private communications or sensitive personal data.
- Technical vulnerabilities and attack methods will be discussed only to the extent necessary to understand the incident and evaluate defensive security measures.
- Security recommendations will be presented as proposed controls based on reported identifiable risks, not as claims that Al Jazeera lack these controls.
- All research findings will be attributed to their respective publicly available sources
- The assessment will distinguish between verified findings, publicly reported information and the researcher's own risk assessment.

## **Assessment Limitations**

This project does not have access to Al Jazeera's internal security policies, network architecture, security configurations incident logs or vulnerability assessments.
